# Data availability

        The Strava activity data used in this study are not publicly available because they are subject to a data-use agreement under the Australian Research Council Linkage Project LP190100089. The SDGSAT-1 nighttime-light data are also restricted under the data-use agreement with the International Research Centre of Big Data for Sustainable Development Goals (CBAS). These restricted datasets cannot be redistributed by the authors.

        All non-restricted derived data, data dictionaries and analysis code are provided in this repository. The public data modules include city metadata and contextual covariates, public built-environment grid covariates, response-curve extraction outputs, response-supported range diagnostics and policy-simulation summary outputs.

        Restricted PA and nighttime-light fields are represented only through schemas, synthetic demo values or aggregate figure-ready outputs. Requests for access to the original Strava and SDGSAT-1 data should be directed to the corresponding data providers and are subject to their data-use agreements.
